Reliable information, then better systems.

I've spent more than six years in data entry, administrative support, and healthcare and inventory operations. Most recently I processed prescription orders for a U.S.-based long-term-care pharmacy, where accuracy and pace both mattered.

Before that, I maintained administrative databases and built Excel-based tracking systems, and worked as an inventory controller keeping stock records accurate. The common thread is information that other people depend on: it only helps when it is accurate, organized, and kept up to date.

That made me curious about the systems around the data: how records, tools, and workflows connect, and where a better process could stop the same problems from repeating. I studied Computer Science, and I'm now learning CRM and workflow tools such as GoHighLevel, along with web and other technical skills. This is a career transition, and I try to be clear about what is experience and what is still learning.
